<a id="1-目的与适用范围"></a>

## 1. Purpose and scope

This document answers: **which datasets does L0 require, and where does each come from?**

L0 validates the engineering workflow and non-bypassable controls, not tax correctness: natural-language creation → interview → fact cards → rules → RAG citations → watermarked report. Data requirements support at least 10 repeatable fictional cases and traceable conclusions without real client data.

It defines required data and acquisition channels, without replacing:

- Capture, parsing, segmentation, validation, and review procedures in the [FSIE extraction/validation plan](../implementation/FSIE_DATA_EXTRACTION_VALIDATION_PLAN.md).
- Schemas/technical choices in [roadmap](TECHNICAL_ROADMAP.md) sections 4–5.
- Data acceptance metrics in [PRD](../product/PRD.md) section 11.

<a id="2-两类数据"></a>

## 2. Two data classes

| Class | Meaning | Sourcing responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| **Preloaded datasets (seed data)** | Prepared before the system runs | Explicit acquisition channels required; this document's focus |
| **Runtime data** | Generated by user actions/system execution | No external source required; schemas must be designed |

Runtime data comprises cases, entities, income events, interview messages, queues, fact cards/versions, rule executions, analysis runs, report versions, human decisions, and audit logs, corresponding to roadmap section 5. It is not detailed here.

## 4. Preloaded datasets

### DS-01 Original legal corpus

Actual Hong Kong FSIE texts required for L0 RAG citations/rule extraction. Retain immutable originals and parsed legal units with provision/page locations and full-text/vector indexes.

| Material | Contents | Required for L0? |
|---|---|---|
| IRO, Cap.112, Part 4 Division 3A (ss.15H–15S) | Core provisions/definitions; HKeL structured XML, current 2026-05-22 version | Required primary legal basis; download verified |
| 2022 FSIE Amendment Ordinance | Legislative amendments/background; text PDF | Required |
| IRD FSIE page | Scope, taxpayers, receipt, exceptions; server-rendered HTML | Required |
| IRD FSIE FAQ | Administrative interpretations/boundaries; server-rendered HTML | Required |
| IRD official examples | Economic substance/participation exemption examples; server-rendered HTML | Required |
| Economic substance advance ruling | ESR advance ruling materials; server-rendered HTML | Required |
| 2023 disposal gains Amendment Ordinance | Disposal gains; text PDF | **Excluded from L0**, `l0_in_scope=false`; registered only, excluded from dividend extraction |

Required source metadata completeness: 100%; every passage locates back to its original. Manifest `legal_coverage_cutoff=2026-09-01`. Hong Kong experts confirm professional completeness before L1; L0 establishes engineering completeness only.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Dividend character | Foreign-sourced dividend | s.15H, s.15I | — | FSIE-INCOME-001 exists |
| 3 | Receipt in Hong Kong | Actual/deemed Hong Kong receipt | s.15I | — | FSIE-RECEIPT-001 exists |
| 4 | Financial entity exclusion | Exempt business of regulated financial entities | s.15H definitions, s.15I exceptions | — | **Missing** |

| 6 | Participation basics | Ownership and continuous holding | **s.15M(2)** | At least 5% continuously for at least 12 months | FSIE-PART-001 exists; thresholds pending |
| 7 | Switchover | Qualifying similar foreign tax; otherwise foreign tax credit route | s.15N(2), 15N(5), **15N(7): 15% reference rate** | 15%, amendable by Gazette notice under 15N(8) | **Missing** |

Each defines required fields/evidence, output enums, dependencies, and cited legal units. Percentages, months, and rates are **candidate values** for executable L0 rules, not professionally validated values. Do not use for real cases before Hong Kong expert confirmation.

<a id="ds-03-事实字段字典"></a>

### DS-03 Fact field dictionary

A common fact vocabulary for rules, evidence, cases, and runtime cards. Each field defines name, type, enums, units (currency/percentage/months/years), nodes, blocking level (scope/conclusion/supporting), and evidence types. Cover PRD FR-03's 8 domains:

1. Recipient, group, special status.
2. Dividend amount/date/character/underlying profit period.
3. Receipt accounts, cash pooling, set-off, payments on behalf, fund paths.
4. Direct/indirect ownership and continuous holding.
5. Foreign taxation/deductibility.
6. Hong Kong personnel, activities, premises, spending, decisions, outsourcing oversight.
7. Establishment, restructuring, commercial purpose, hybrid mismatches.
8. Evidence documents.

This is the most critical current gap: rules.json, evidence_requirements.json, and candidate_cases.json use inconsistent names, e.g. `continuous_holding_period` versus `continuous_holding_period_months`. Standardize them before engine consumption.

<a id="ds-04-证据要求目录"></a>

### DS-04 Evidence requirements

Define evidence types (business registration, group charts, bank statements, shareholder registers, foreign tax receipts, etc.) and their fact/node mappings. L0 accepts no actual files; cases use `provided / missing / conflict / not_applicable` with fictional descriptions.

## 6. Original legal source channels and validation

<a id="61-两类渠道走不同解析管线"></a>

### 6.1 Two channels, separate pipelines

**IRO structured channel (preferred).** Department of Justice HKeL provides machine-readable files:

- Chapter index: `https://resource.data.one.gov.hk/doj/data/hkel_list_c_all_en.xml`, downloaded 2026-09-01, 2,834,277 bytes, HTTP 200.
- Current Cap.112 in `https://resource.data.one.gov.hk/doj/data/hkel_c_leg_cap_1_cap_300_en.zip`, combined Cap.1–300, 235,917,074 bytes. File `cap_112_en_c/cap_112_20260522000000_en_c.xml`, 6,559,279 bytes, is the current consolidated IRO.
- Web entry: `https://www.elegislation.gov.hk/hk/cap112!en`, retaining historical versions since 1997-06-30.

Section/subsection/paragraph hierarchies are structured. **Parse XML separately without OCR.** Extract only `cap_112_en_c` from the large archive, not the whole package into the database.

**IRD administrative webpage/PDF channel.** IRD has no open API for FSIE legislative content; its data.gov.hk datasets concern administrative information such as office addresses. Capture manifest-listed pages/FAQs/examples/rulings as HTML and amendments as PDFs. Tests on 2026-09-01 found 4 server-rendered pages with substantive economic substance/participation text without browser rendering, and 2 digital text PDFs with font objects/no image XObjects. OCR is not expected, but Iteration 2 must verify lossless extraction with the selected parser.

<a id="8-l0-明确不包含的数据"></a>

## 8. Explicit L0 exclusions

- No real client cases, personal data, or client evidence files.
- No commercial legal databases or machine translations as professional evidence.
- No professionally verified rule status; mandatory research watermarks.
- No interest, IP, disposal gains, or other income types; the 2023 disposal amendment is registered out of scope.
- No in-depth mainland China/Japan/South Korea rules; their 2026 delivery is source-catalog baseline.
- PRD schedules upload for P2; L0 evidence is simulated through DS-04 enums.
