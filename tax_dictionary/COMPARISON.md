## Reading coverage

`rules_available` means compiled rules exist for specific company scenarios; it does not mean the entire topic is implemented. `not_modelled` is never a zero tax rate.

Every jurisdiction/topic entry has independent source, structure, model, judgement and completeness statuses in [coverage.csv](data/coverage.csv). Cross-topic document counts overlap.
