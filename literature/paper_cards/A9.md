# Programming Languages and Law: A Research Agenda

## 1. 研究问题与作者主张

- 目标：作者称本文 "surveys the history of research into programming languages and law and presents ten promising avenues for future efforts" [p.1]；目的是说明 programming languages (PL) 与 law 的结合在更广的 computer science and law 领域中有何 distinctive 之处，并用具体例子展示 "the remarkable power of programming-language concepts in this new domain" [p.1]。
- 核心主张（作者认为）："If code is law, then the language of law is a programming language" [p.1]；律师和法学者可通过研究 PL 理论了解法律，PL 工具可有用地应用于法律问题 [p.1]。作者称本文一方面响应 Paul Ohm 与 Houman Shadab 的呼吁（PL 理论可帮助法学者写出更好的代码），另一方面提出 "a more ambitious claim"：若 code is law，则理解 code 是理解 law 的一种方式，反之亦然 [p.2]。
- PL 的独特之处（作者认为）：律师与程序员使用词语的方式相似，computer science 与 law 都是 linguistic professions，程序文本与法律文本之间有 "a nearly exact analogy" [p.1]；AI 处理 legal structures，NLP 处理 legal language，而作者称只有 PL theory 提供在律师创造法律结构所用语言表达层面分析这些结构的 principled, systematic framework [p.1]。
- 两种意义（作者认为）：作者区分 CS 子领域对法律的两类作用，即回应法律面临的紧迫问题与提供看待法律的新视角；主张 PL 理论在两种意义上都相关：对某些实际法律问题 "indispensable"，也能 "illuminate the entire law"（引自参考文献 36）[p.1]。
- 结论性表述：作者称从该领域历史中可得的经验是 "principled methods are often superior to ad hoc ones" [p.9]，并称把一个 body of law 形式化到足以变成 programming language 的程度 "forces you to understand it in a far deeper way" [p.9]。

## 2. 方法

- 文章性质：作者把本文称为 article，内容是综述 PL 与 law 的研究史并提出十个研究方向 [p.1]；文中有 forward-looking 的部分，也有 backward-looking 的部分 [p.2]；所列方向 "Some of them are being actively pursued, others are promising speculations" [p.2]。
- 结构：§2 三个 case studies（2.1 Legal Logic Programming Languages、2.2 The French Tax Code、2.3 Future Interests）[pp.2–3]；§3 十个 research topics（3.1–3.10）[pp.3–8]；§4 Closing Thoughts [pp.8–9]。作者称三个案例分别说明：CS 与 law 的其他进展常建立在 PL 理论之上；PL 理论进路改进 legal technology 实践；PL 方法自然适合许多法律问题 [p.2]。
- 论证方式：描述既有项目，把 PL 概念与法律实务现象作类比，列举涉及软件的判例与条文，对照两个学科的工作方式 [pp.2–9]；每个研究方向标题下有一句概括句（3.3 为问句）[pp.3–8]。
- 文中没有作者自己的形式化模型、算法、实验设计或实证策略 [pp.1–9]；选取案例和方向的标准、文献检索方式，原文未说明。文中给出一段 M++ 项目的代码片段（compute_benefits）作为税务计算示例，未逐行解释 [pp.2–3]。

## 4. 结果

### 4.1 实证结果

- 文中没有作者自己的实验、测量、数据分析或评测结果；全文为综述与研究议程 [pp.1–9]。
- 文中出现的数字属于对既有工作或背景事实的描述，例如：法国税法 "about 3,500 pages of text" [p.2]；Composing Contracts 的 "minimal set of ten combinators" [p.4]。

### 4.2 三个 case studies：作者对既有工作的描述

- 2.1 Legal logic programming languages：作者称 AI and law 的主要研究线之一是 legal logic programming；通用 logic programming languages 不总能刻画法律推理，因为许多法律结论是 defeasible，而 Prolog 等语言的语义是 monotonic，刻画前者 "can require cumbersome circumlocutions"；为此出现了把特定特性加入语言本身的 legal programming languages：Catala（defeasible reasoning）、PROLEG（default reasoning）、s(LAW)（以多个答案表示 legal ambiguity 与 administrative discretion）、LLD（temporal 与 deontic operators）[p.2]。
- 2.2 French tax code：作者称 DGFiP 须把法国税法转换为每位纳税人的应缴金额，所用程序依赖 legacy custom language [M] 及其编译器，该系统 "fragile and hard to maintain, and may contain bugs and mistakes" [p.2]。一组研究者与 DGFiP 合作，已 reverse engineered M 并给出 formal semantics，开发了 M++（作者称有 "better formal properties and cleaner syntax"）与支持 M 和 M++ 的编译器 MLang，并在用它 "speed up, audit, debug, and ultimately prove correct the algorithmic implementation of the tax laws" [p.2]；文中未报告 MLang 在速度、审计或调试方面的具体结果 [pp.2–3]。作者称表层含义是 MLang "will make tax computations more reliable"，深层含义是 M++ 使人能以新方式理解、推理和辩论法国税法 [p.3]。
- 2.3 Future interests：Orlando 是 real estate 中 future interests 的 programming language，surface syntax 是律师书写 conveyances 所用结构的 stylized subset；Littleton 把这类 quasi-natural-language 的 programs 编译为 tree-based data structure，如 "O conveys to A for life, then to B so long as B does not marry" 产生 A 的 life estate、B 的 remainder、O 的 possibility of reverter 三个 interests；Orlando 有 operational semantics，说明 interests 随事件的变化；Littleton 能对 tree 推理，如正确命名 interests、应用 Rule Against Perpetuities [p.3]。作者称 Orlando 可作教学工具，其 syntax 与 semantics 本身是 "a scholarly claim about the underlying structure of the law of future interests"；Littleton 不是可视化 future interests 的唯一工具 [p.3]。

### 4.3 十个研究方向：作者的论述

- 3.1 Legal domain-specific languages：作者称 law 整体 "much too large for a DSL (at least for now)"，许多子领域大小合适；适合的领域是法律文本已部分接近程序者，有三个特征：用 rules 而非 standards、参与者高度重视 clarity、有 recurring patterned structures；transactional private-law 领域是 "the most obvious low-hanging fruit"，如 contract DSLs、will drafting、IP licensing [p.3]。
- 3.2 Hybrid contracts：作者称 contracts 是 "One of the most promising domains for legal DSLs"，部分因为此处已有最多工作；作者称既有合同建模研究与 blockchain smart contracts 带来的 PL 兴趣的汇合 "suggests that there may be substantial conceptual and practical payoffs" 于结合 human-readable terms 与 machine-readable logic 的系统 [p.3]。例：Carla Reyes 的表格同时生成可在 blockchain 上运行的 smart contract 和可向州办公室提交的 financing statement [pp.3–4]；作者称这类做法 "could help solve" 代码与可读摘要不一致的问题；形式化可形式化的部分有助于防止或至少发现逻辑 bug；好的 PL 驱动进路 "would help enormously" 解决其 doctrinal 问题 [p.4]。
- 3.3 Orthogonal legal primitives（一）：作者问哪些 legal concepts 足够简单、普遍，可作 legal PL 的 building blocks；称对法律推理特点的建模中有 "a striking underlying simplicity"：Orlando 把 conveyance 效果归结为 interests 的终止与排序，Catala 在 lambda calculus 上只增加 exceptions；作者称 Composing Contracts 的语言是 "The crown jewel of legal calculi"，其 "minimal set of ten combinators is both powerful and elegant" [p.4]。
- 3.3（二）：作者提出 "A profitable line of research would be to identify additional legal primitives and to systematize their relationships to each other"；已有例子含 sequencing 与 termination、conjunction 与 disjunction、defaults 与 exceptions、obligations 与 entitlements；其他可能适合译为 PL 特性的概念含 business associations 中实体的创建与组合、hierarchies of authority、commercial law 的 transfer 与 notice 等 [p.4]。作者列三个问题：clean minimal formalizations（引 Jones 等称找到 "right" primitive combinators "quite a challenge"）；orthogonality（obligation 与 transfer 不完全 orthogonal，因义务是否 "run with the land" 有时是有时否）；不同 legal PLs 的共性是否足以 justify 在现成 back end 中加入 law-specific 特性，作者写 "I do not know the answer, but the question seems worth asking" [p.4]。
- 3.4 Legal drafting languages：作者写 "Perhaps PL theory can do the same for law" [p.4]，即像为程序员提供促进清晰、正确代码的语言特性那样为法律起草提供这类特性。例：Lawsky 观察到 statutory definitions 的范围常有歧义，建议起草者用 first-order predicate logic 形式化定义以明确范围；作者称 "It is actually impossible to draft a tax provision in first-order logic with ambiguous scope"，形式化条文再 compiled 为自然语言的过程 "hopefully will not introduce errors of this sort" [p.5]。作者另列五类可能的 PL 特性：variables and binding、cross-referencing、counterfactuals and hypotheticals、substitution、abstraction [p.5]。
- 3.5 Legal design patterns：作者说明 software 中的 design pattern（定义见第 6 节）[p.5]；称法律起草中的 design patterns 有 "nascent literature"，提出 "A more ambitious agenda would seek to craft pattern languages for specific legal areas"；举英国 Office of the Parliamentary Counsel 的 Common Legislative Solutions 为 "A promising example"，其按 Pattern Language 格式给出 pattern 描述、sub-elements 清单和实例 [p.6]。
- 3.6 Design principles：作者以 modularity 为 design principles 的 "A key example"，称其理论源于 cybernetics，常借助 PL 特性实现；Henry Smith 展示 property 的边界常以 modular 方式划定以管理 information costs [p.6]。作者称 recursion、compositionality、type-safety、extensibility 等原则也可能相关，法律起草已 "ad hoc" 使用，系统化可能使起草者更精确；举例：合同条款分为 representations 与 promises 属于 typing，Federal Rules of Evidence 对 privilege rules 的授权是 "an extensibility framework" [p.6]。
- 3.7 An IDE for lawyers：作者称律师最常见的 IDE 是 Microsoft Word，最常见的 version-control 是以 "RMD Agreement 3.2 MH Final 2 USE THIS ONE" 之类文件名保存草稿；作者写 "the IDE lags behind" [pp.6–7]。作者设想的 drafting IDE 功能包括：syntax highlighting；linting 与 type-checking 等 static analyses；检测 law smells；与 version control 等系统集成；unit tests 与 fuzz testing；interactive simulation 与 debugging 工具 [p.7]。
- 3.8 Jupyter notebooks for law：作者称 PL 方法常提高 understanding，对比 subsymbolic AI 以 explainability problem 著称，而 PL theory "for the most part" 不然；讨论 visualization、interactivity、literate legal drafting（Catala 项目的 pair programming；Accord Project 的 Cicero）[p.7]。作者称 "An ambitious goal" 是造出法律领域的 Jupyter notebook：自由混排面向人的文本与可执行片段，可视化法律结构，把反馈缩短到 "a matter of seconds or less" [p.7]。
- 3.9 The law of software：作者称程序虽不像医学那样是受监管的职业，但在许多方面受法律约束，并列出九个答案部分取决于 PL 理论者专长的 doctrinal 问题 [p.7]：专利适格性（是否 "abstract idea"、是否增加 "significantly more"）；专利侵权（Supreme Court 认为 Windows 的 CD-ROM 不是专利发明的 component）[pp.7–8]；版权对 literary works 的保护与 Scratch 等 visual PL、版权不延及的元素、复制 API 属 fair use 与 interface/implementation 的划分；First Amendment 对软件发布与功能性使用的划线；Apple 主张被要求写代码解锁 iPhone 违反 compelled speech，作者称若要求打开实体保险库该主张 "would obviously fail"；smart contracts 的解释，作者称法院 "will need a similarly rich theory"；Computer Fraud and Abuse Act 禁止 "without authorization" 使用计算机，作者称在某些情形下何为 authorization 由程序自身决定 [p.8]。
- 3.10 Philosophical questions：作者称 legal 与 PL 学者都研究 interpretation，二者共同关注文本及其意义；"software consist entirely of mathematics and is therefore not properly patentable or copyrightable" 的主张建立在 PL semantics 理论之上，但这 "is not the only possible way to conceive of software" [p.8]。作者提出一个理论问题（人与计算机解读文本有何不同），称其答案显示法律与 PL 能做什么，也显示由 "unbridgeable differences" 决定的不能做什么；一个实践问题（计算机的可得性是否、如何改变法律文本的写作与解释），称这是法律职业 "urgently" 需要自问的问题 [p.8]。

### 4.4 作者的解释或推论

- 作者认为，AI and law 中许多情形下解决问题的最佳办法一直是创建 programming language，这 "is a sign that there are aspects of legal reasoning that programming languages are uniquely well-suited to capture" [p.2]；称 M++ 案例是 "a classic programming-languages story about replacing an ad hoc system with one that rests on a firm theoretical and engineering foundation" [p.3]。
- 作者认为两个领域都关注专业人士如何用精确结构化的语言构造做事，在 CS 中最与法律共享这一语言关注的是 PL；两个领域在研究方法、rigor 标准等方面不同，应对办法包括合作与 CS-law 双重训练，后者 "although rare, is immensely helpful in bridging the two fields" [pp.8–9]。

## 5. 作者陈述的局限与前提

文中没有专门的局限小节；以下为行文中作者给出的限定、开放问题和前提。

- 前提：作者以 "if code is law" 为出发点 [p.2]，并断言程序文本与法律文本之间有 "a nearly exact analogy" [p.1]。
- 范围限定：作者称 law 整体 "much too large for a DSL (at least for now)" [p.3]；所列方向 "Some of them are being actively pursued, others are promising speculations" [p.2]；law of software 的问题只是 "just a few of the many" [p.7]。
- 开放问题：不同 legal PLs 的共性是否足以 justify 在现成 back end 中加入 law-specific 特性，作者写 "I do not know the answer" [p.4]；obligation 与 transfer 不完全 orthogonal，作者称 "This is a hard problem" [p.4]；形式化条文 compiled 为自然语言的步骤 "hopefully will not introduce errors of this sort" [p.5]。
- 规范性限定：作者主张 "some (not all) legal rules should be made more formal" [p.8]；对计算机承担更多起草及适用法律的可能，写 "just because something can be done, doesn't mean it should" [p.8]。
- 跨学科的难处：作者称建立该领域 "will not be easy"，学者在自己领域之外可能是 "overconfident amateurs" [p.8]。

## 6. 关键术语

- defeasible 与 monotonic：作者称法律结论 defeasible，即 "they are valid on the basis of present knowledge, but could fail if a known exception turns out to be the case"；classical logic 是 monotonic，即 "once a conclusion is established, it can never be falsified by additional hypotheses" [p.2]。
- domain-specific language (DSL)：作者称 DSL "is specialized to meet the challenges of a specific problem domain" [p.3]。
- legal calculus：作者引 Basu 等人（参考文献 15）："a language with well-defined syntax and semantics that has features whose main purpose is to model particular aspects of its [legal] target domain" [p.4]。
- primitive 与 orthogonal：特性应 primitive，即 "it cannot be further decomposed"；特性之间应 orthogonal，即 "they have simple and easily predictable interactions" [p.4]。
- design pattern：软件工程中 "a general, reusable template for solutions to a commonly occurring class of design problems" [p.5]。
- modularity：作者定义为 "the decomposition of a system into subsystems that are weakly coupled to each other" [p.6]。

## 7. 原文摘录

- "If code is law, then the language of law is a programming language. Lawyers and legal scholars can learn about law by studying programming-language theory, and programming-language tools can be usefully applied to legal problems." [p.1]
- "But only PL theory provides a principled, systematic framework to analyze legal structures in terms of the linguistic expressions lawyers use to create them." [p.1]
- "This article will review three case studies of the use of PL theory for law, and then present ten promising avenues of potential research. Some of them are being actively pursued, others are promising speculations." [p.2]
- "'Law' as a whole is much too large for a DSL (at least for now), but many legal subfields are the right size for a DSL." [p.3]
- "Is there enough in common among different legal PLs to justify adding law-specific features (e.g. for defaults or obligations) to one of those off-the-shelf back ends? I do not know the answer, but the question seems worth asking." [p.4]
- "I have argued that some (not all) legal rules should be made more formal, and that the legal drafting process should be more like the software development process. ... Of course, just because something can be done, doesn't mean it should." [p.8]
- "If there is a lesson to take from the history of law and programming languages’s engagement, it is that principled methods are often superior to ad hoc ones." [p.9]
