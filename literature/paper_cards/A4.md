# A Logic for Statutes

## 1. 研究问题与作者主张

- 问题与范围：作者考察 statutory reasoning 这类 rule-based legal reasoning 在歧义已消解、条文用语含义已确定之后的结构（after ambiguities are resolved and the meaning of the statute's terms established）[p.2]。作者称，法学著述集中于 case-based / common law reasoning，rule-based legal reasoning 被视为 simple，词义确定之后 statutory reasoning 的结构只有 'little treatment' [pp.2–3]。
- 核心主张：作者主张 standard formal logic 不是对 statutory rule-based reasoning 建模的 'best approach' [p.2]；以 Internal Revenue Code 及相关 regulations、judicial decisions、rulings 为主要例子，主张至少一部分（at least some）rule-based legal reasoning 最适合刻画为 defeasible reasoning，并用 nonstandard logic 中的 default logic 建模 [pp.3–4]。作者限定主张的强度：default logic 不是 'required' 而是 'preferable'，且是否采用是 'a question of pragmatics' [p.16]。
- 准确性主张：作者称 default logic 'is superior to standard logic for representing certain statutory reasoning'：它使隐含的 rule priority 推理与决定显式化；捕捉 legal rules 的结构（standard logic 对此 'entirely fails to do'）；更准确反映律师、法官和立法起草者实际进行的 rule-based legal reasoning [p.4]。
- 益处主张：作者称有三项 theoretical and practical advantages：(1) 凸显并使人能对 rule priority 进行理论化；(2) 更容易把 (legal) code 译为 (computer) code，作者将此与 AI 在法律实务中的使用（如 e-discovery）相联系；(3) 立法起草者形式化其实际做法后，'will find it simpler to detect errors and ambiguities in legislation' [p.4]。
- 与已有文献的关系：作者称有一批文献（Hage、Prakken & Sartor、Sartor）主张 legal reasoning 最好理解为 defeasible reasoning，但这些来源 'generally (though not entirely) neglect the intrinsic defeasibility of rule-based legal reasoning' [p.4]；作者称 Walker 的讨论限于证据（fact-finding）推理 [pp.4–5]，Horty 用联邦法与州法冲突的虚构例子；本文改用真实法条，展示 'defeasibility intrinsic to the statute itself' [p.5]。

## 2. 方法

- 性质：论证性文章（作者称 Essay）。Part II 用非法律的 Henry 例子说明形式系统；Part III 把 I.R.C. § 163 写成 default rules 并演示一次推导；Part IV 分 'accuracy' 与 'benefits' 论证为何用 default logic [pp.2–3] [p.12]。
- 概念与形式系统：deductive reasoning 的结论 indefeasible，其形式化 standard logic 是 monotonic；defeasible reasoning 的结论可被新信息推翻，其形式化是 nonmonotonic（例：Tweety 是 bird 则推出能飞，得知是 penguin 则改为不能飞）[p.5]。作者使用 'a variant of default logic'（脚注 9：最初由 Reiter 1980 提出）：命题公式集 W（可非正式地看作 a world of facts）、default rules 的集合 D（每条 δ）、default rules 之间的关系 <，合记 Δ = (W, D, <) [p.5]；< 确定相对优先级，故为 prioritized default logic [pp.5–6]。规则越 'lower' 越强：δ1 < δ2 时 δ1 dominates δ2，两者都可能适用时 δ1 'beats' δ2 [p.6]。
- 推导过程：(1) 接受 W 及用 monotonic standard logic 能从 W 证明的一切；(2) 按优先级从最 dominant 的规则起逐条检查：与已接受内容不矛盾则加入该规则及可推出的一切，若会既推出某命题又推出其否定则不加入；(3) 无规则可查时，接受可由 W 与已加入规则推出的一切 [pp.6–7]。
- Henry 例：Young 指 younger than age six，Henry 五岁；W = {UnitedStates, Young}；δ2: UnitedStates → Read；δ1: Young → ¬Read；δ1 < δ2 [p.6]。
- § 163 的形式化：对象为 § 163(h)（home mortgage interest deduction），作者称其 'nothing unusual'，选它的理由是既复杂又 'fairly brief' [p.7]；δ3: Interest → Deductible；δ2: Personal → ¬Deductible；δ1: QRI → Deductible；W = {Personal → Interest, QRI → Personal}（作者称这两条 certain，essentially definitional）；< : δ1 < δ2 < δ3 [pp.9–11]。作者写道 'One might argue' 还有第四条规则 δ0: Payment → ¬Deductible（并把 Interest → Payment 放入 W），但 'for our purposes, these three suffice' [p.11]。
- 对 δ1 的处理：作者写道 δ1 'may seem' 与 Code 不符（Code 把 qualified residence interest 放在 personal interest 定义的排除项中），但 'notwithstanding the language of the Code'，qualified residence interest 在概念上是 personal interest 不可扣除规则的例外，并以 Pau v. Commissioner、一部 treatise 和 1985 年税改建议为例，称这些例子 'selected almost at random' [pp.10–11]；脚注 22：§ 163 内没有规则表明 δ1 是 defeasible，但 Code 其他规则可能击败它 [p.10]。
- 演示：W = {Personal → Interest, QRI → Personal, Personal}，即个人利息支付且 'no evidence that the payment is qualified residence interest'；依次考察 δ1、δ2、δ3 [p.12]。

## 4. 结果

**实证结果**

- 原文没有实验、统计检验或评测数值；全文由概念论证和对 § 163 的形式化示例构成 [pp.2–19]。

**形式化示例的推导输出**（作者的演示，不是实证数据）

- Henry 例：在 W = {UnitedStates, Young}、δ1 < δ2 下，作者得出 Henry 不能读（¬Read）：δ1 先加入，δ2 因会使 Read 与 ¬Read 同时可推出而不加入 [pp.6–7]。
- § 163 例：对个人利息且没有 QRI 证据的支付，作者得出该支付不可扣除（isn't deductible）：δ1、δ2 依次与已有信念一致而加入，δ3 因与已接受的 δ2 及事实 Personal 不一致而不加入 [p.12]；原文中逐步推导的 § 163 情形只有这一种 [pp.9–12]。

**作者对 § 163 的数字说明**（法条适用的算例，不是推导输出）

- 公司例：借款 $10,000、年利率 8%，每年利息 $800；税率 35% 时税额减少 $280（35% of $800）[p.7]。
- Benny 例：房价 $1.6 million，现金 $500,000，购房抵押贷款 $1.1 million，利率 7%，每年利息 $77,000；acquisition indebtedness 上限 $1 million，作者算得可扣除 $70,000 [p.8]；'he will save $25,830 (39.6% of $70,000)'（见第 8 节）[p.9]。
- 第二例：$700,000 的住宅全以房屋担保债务购入，利率 5%，每年可扣除 $35,000，税额减少 $13,860（39.6% of $35,000）；房价升至 $800,000 后再借的 $100,000 为 home equity indebtedness；借 $150,000 仍只能就 $100,000 的差额扣除利息 [p.9]。

**作者的解释或推论**

- 作者写道 'some metarule is required to know how to apply apparently conflicting statutory rules'：§ 163(a) 没有 'except as otherwise stated in this Section' 一类措辞（对照 § 61），§ 163(h) 'seems, again, strictly speaking, inconsistent' with § 163(a) [pp.12–13]；作者称此处冲突 'not difficult to resolve'（canons 要求 more specific rule 优先于 more general rule），但 'simply on the statute's face, deductive logic is not sufficient to represent the rules' [p.13]。
- 作者称其他情形答案 'less obvious'，如 Service 不 acquiesce Tax Court 判决时，'the taxpayer-favorable revenue ruling controls'，各 authority 的相对强度 'for the most part' 不包含在被解释的 statute 中 [p.13]；作者称 Dworkin 论规则冲突的一段话 'is exactly the approach of defeasible reasoning and default logic'，并称被放弃的规则与其说 'not valid'，'perhaps more accurate' 的说法是被另一规则 dominated 而可能不适用 [pp.13–14]。
- 作者称 'The Code is not flat'，举 2013 年设立的 NIIT 位于 Chapter 2A 因而不适用 foreign tax credit（§ 901 限于 Chapter 1 所征之税）和 social security totalization treaties（限于 Chapter 2 所征之税），称由此产生的 double tax 'entirely due to the location of the NIIT in the Code' [p.14]。
- 作者写道 'not surprising that default logic accurately reflects the statutory structure'，因为 general-to-specific 是立法起草者被推荐的做法：House 手册（1995）按 GENERAL RULE、EXCEPTIONS、SPECIAL RULES，Senate 手册（1997）按 DEFINITIONS、GENERAL RULE、EXCEPTIONS、SPECIAL RULES [p.15]；Revenue Ruling 2010–25 依次陈述 general rule、第一个 exception（personal interest 不可扣除）和最后一个 exception（QRI 可扣除），作者称 'any textbook, treatise, or case' 亦如此 [p.16]。
- 作者写道 'a nonmonotonic logic such as default logic isn't necessary for representing defeasible reasoning'：对 § 163，standard monotonic logic 的一条规则 Interest → (Deductible ↔ (¬Deductible v QRI)) 即可得 'the correct answer (deductible or not deductible)'（公式照原文抄录，见第 8 节）；脚注 34 称它 'isn't an exhaustively accurate statement of the law'；作者称 Dworkin 提出规则是 'all-or-nothing'，'accurate' 的规则陈述把所有 exceptions 纳入考虑；作者另写道，在 nonmonotonic 与 monotonic logic 间选择 'perhaps' 是 'a false choice'，其他 nonstandard logics（脚注 36：Nolt et al. 1995）'may actually be better suited' [p.16]。
- 作者称 'What logic is best depends on one's purpose'：以 Nolt 的目的（让 AI 得出准确的法律结论）而言，逻辑是否反映 statutory structure 'of little interest'，对 rule-based legal reasoning 则 'the pragmatics is on the side of default logic'；default logic 与 'standard nonmonotonic logic'、'other nonstandard logics' 相比 'preferable'（用词见第 8 节）[p.16]。
- rule priority：作者称 default logic 使 rule priority 这一 'conceptual category' 凸显，该范畴 'remains undertheorized'；作者写道某些 priority 'seem obvious'，但执法者表示不执行时 'the enforcer's notice dominates the statute'：Notice 2008–76 中 Service 宣布不对 banks 执行 § 382 的一项规定，作者描述其效果为 'effectively transferring over $100 billion to certain private parties in violation of explicit, clear statutory law'，并写道 'No lawyer would advise his client to follow the statute and not the Notice' [p.17]。
- 作者称 'right answer' 可指 'the answer that is most compliant with the law'、'the advice that a tax lawyer should give a risk-averse client' 或 'the conclusion a judge would reach'；default logic 的形式化可给出其中任何一种，取决于 formalizer 赋予各规则的 priority，本文取第二种 [p.17]。
- 转译为代码：作者称把 statutes 转为 default logic 比转为 standard logic 更容易：对 § 163 抽取三条 default rules 'is straightforward'，每条可引到具体 subsection，而在 standard logic 下写出单条规则 'requires applying metarules and deviating from the statutory structure' [pp.17–18]；作者称基于 default logic 的 AI 更容易编码和抽取 statutes 的信息，可给规则标注优先级而不必手工合并 [p.18]；脚注 40 称 Pertierra et al.（July 2017）是用 natural language processing 从税法抽取 default-logic rules 的 'initial attempt' [p.18]。
- 起草检查：作者称 drafters 'may be more likely' 用形式化检查法条结构，'which might help avoid errors and unintentional ambiguities'；例：acquisition indebtedness 的定义是否含 $1 million 限额；因 definitions are extensional，两种读法对 home equity indebtedness 的计算不同：若含，借 $1.1 million 购房者前 $1 million 的利息按 acquisition indebtedness、最后 $100,000 的利息按 home equity indebtedness 扣除；若不含，原文写作最后 $100,000 'cannot be home acquisition indebtedness'（见第 8 节）[p.18]。
- 作者称该问题 'has been extensively litigated'：government 起初主张定义不含该限额，因而最后 $100,000 不构成 home equity indebtedness，并在 Pau v. Commissioner 中胜诉；Service 后来放弃该立场，允许第二个 $100,000 计为 home equity indebtedness [pp.18–19]。作者写道，若起草者曾形式化其法条，'would have had to decide' 定义包含什么，并会解决 'what seems to be an unintentional ambiguity' [p.19]。
- 结论：formal logic 'is a useful way to model rule-based legal reasoning if one uses a nonstandard logic'；以 § 163 为例，作者主张 certain types of rule-based legal reasoning 是 defeasible reasoning 且 'best modeled by default logic'；本文 'suggests' 使用 default logic 的理论与实践优势 [p.19]。

## 5. 作者陈述的局限与前提

文中未专门讨论局限；以下是作者在行文中写明的范围限定、前提与让步。

- 范围：只讨论歧义已消解、词义已确定之后的推理结构 [pp.2–3]；主张的措辞是 'at least some' [p.3] 和 'certain types of' [p.19] rule-based legal reasoning。
- 替代做法：对 § 163 一条 monotonic 规则即可得出正确答案，其他 nonstandard logics 'may actually be better suited'，是否用 default logic 是 'a question of pragmatics' [p.16]。
- 规则排序的前提：'right answer' 取 'the advice that a tax lawyer should give a risk-averse client'，规则据此排序 [p.17]。
- 形式化的简化：对术语作形式定义 'adds a level of complication'，但这些规则 'at heart' 提供 § 163(h) 的结构 [p.11]；脚注 34：那条 monotonic 规则 'isn't an exhaustively accurate statement of the law' [p.16]。
- 规则性质与措辞：default rules 'don't apply with certainty, but in general they are good guides to reasoning' [p.6]；drafters 'may be more likely' 使用形式化，脚注 40 所引工作是 'an initial attempt' [p.18]；作者称 § 163 'nothing unusual' [p.7]，'in no way unique' [p.12]。

## 6. 关键术语

- defeasible reasoning / indefeasible：defeasible conclusions 是 'conclusions that might be defeated by additional information'；deductive reasoning 的结论 'cannot be defeated by additional information'，故 indefeasible [p.5]。
- monotonic / nonmonotonic（standard logic）：deductive logic 的形式化称 'standard logic'，是 monotonic，即结论一经接受不能拒绝；形式化的 defeasible logic 是 nonmonotonic，新信息可使推理者拒绝先前的结论 [p.5]。
- default logic 与 Δ = (W, D, <)：'a variant of default logic'（注为源自 Reiter 1980）；W 为命题公式集，D 为 default rules 之集，< 为 default rules 之间的关系，建立 'the relative priority of the default rules—which rule takes precedence over another'，故称 prioritized default logic [pp.5–6]；规则越 'lower' 越强，δ1 < δ2 时 δ1 dominates δ2，两者都可能适用时 δ1 'beats' δ2 [p.6]。
- metarule：作者用法为 'some metarule is required to know how to apply apparently conflicting statutory rules'；原文未给出独立定义 [p.12]。
- personal interest / qualified residence interest (QRI)：personal interest payments 以排除法定义：除六项之外的所有 payments，这六项大致包括经营目的债务的利息和 qualified residence interest [p.7]；QRI 指就 qualified residence 的 acquisition indebtedness 或 home equity indebtedness 所生的利息 [p.8]。
- acquisition indebtedness / home equity indebtedness：前者为取得、建造或 substantially improving qualified residence 而负担且以该住宅担保的债务，某年最高 $1 million [p.8]；后者以 qualified residence 担保，限于该住宅 fair market value 减去其 acquisition indebtedness 的部分，总额不得超过 $100,000 [p.9]。

## 7. 原文摘录

- "It argues that standard formal logic is not the best approach for modeling statutory rule-based reasoning. Rather, ... that at least some statutory reasoning is best characterized as defeasible reasoning ... and is best modeled using default logic." [p.2]
- "Default logic is superior to standard logic for representing certain statutory reasoning because, as the Essay explains, default logic makes explicit otherwise implicit reasoning and decisions about rule priority, decisions that are required to follow statutes and other legal rules." [p.4]
- "That said, a nonmonotonic logic such as default logic isn't necessary for representing defeasible reasoning. Take section 163, for example. One arrives at the correct answer (deductible or not deductible) with just one rule, in standard monotonic logic" [p.16]
- "But my claim isn't that default logic is required to represent rule-based legal reasoning, but rather that default logic is preferable for formalizing such reasoning, as compared both to standard nonmonotonic logic and to other nonstandard logics." [p.16]
- "Had the drafters formalized their statute to check its structure, they would have had to decide what was included in the definition of acquisition indebtedness and would have resolved what seems to be an unintentional ambiguity." [p.19]
