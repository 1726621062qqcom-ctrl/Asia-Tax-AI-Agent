# TaxCalcBench: Evaluating Frontier Models on the Tax Calculation Task

## 1. 研究问题与作者主张

- 研究问题：作者提出 TaxCalcBench，考察模型在已给出全部必要信息的条件下计算美国个人所得税申报表的能力 [p.1]。作者的出发点是：近一年 LLM 在 coding 与 math 任务上有所提升（作者以 AIME、LiveCodeBench 等基准为例），而 "we haven't seen LLMs formally tested on their ability to calculate taxes" [pp.1–2]。
- 主要实证主张：作者报告 "state-of-the-art models succeed in calculating less than a third of federal income tax returns even on this simplified sample set" [p.1]。
- 关于失败方式：作者称其分析 "concludes that models consistently misuse tax tables, make errors in tax calculation, and incorrectly determine eligibility" [p.1]。
- 关于后续需求：作者认为结果 "point to the need for additional infrastructure to apply LLMs to the personal income tax calculation task" [p.1]，并写 "a continued need for deterministic tax calculation engines to ensure accuracy" [p.2]。
- 优先性陈述：作者写 "To our knowledge, this is the first AI benchmark of this type for the tax calculation task" [p.13]。

## 2. 方法

- 任务设定：作者把报税分为 Document collection、Preparation、Calculation 三个子任务，该基准 "solely focused on (3) Calculation"，即把已录入的信息转换为完成的申报表（个人所得税为 Form 1040）[p.2]。作者称传统上由各 tax software provider 把以英文定义的 IRS、州和地方规则写成 deterministic 的 tax calculation engines，并认为难点在规则的数量与相互关联，以及没有 IRS 或各州给出的 'answer key' [pp.2–3]。
- 基准构成：51 对 user inputs 与预期正确输出，加一个把模型输出与预期结果比较的 testing harness；输入为 proprietary JSON，含完整计算申报表所需的全部信息，作者据此认为 Document collection 与 Preparation 可视为已 100% 正确完成；预期输出为 IRS 要求的 MeF XML [p.4]。
- 提示与输出：模型被提示在给定全部 user inputs 的条件下计算申报表，输出简化的纯文本格式（每行 `Line N: [Description] | [Explanation of calculations, if any] | [Amount]`），而非 MeF XML；作者给出的理由是 models "can't yet natively produce MeF schema-compatible XML"，脚注称 XML schemas 太大，放不进 context [p.5]。所测的是模型 "natively calculate a correct tax return for the 2024 Tax Year" 的能力 [p.5]；原文未说明是否提供工具。
- 提示词：作者写 'The prompt matters'，称试验提示以找到自认为公平的一个；最终提示含五项特征：说明模型在帮助测试税务计算软件（作者写原因是测试时模型默认安全机制有时会拒绝准备或计算它认为是真实的申报表）、要求计算主 Form 1040 及所需的其他表单与附表、允许跳过 SSN 字段、完整说明输出格式（含 Form 1040 的逐行说明）、说明输入数据格式 [p.7]。作者另写提示中为模型预先给出 "the correct Form 1040 lines and short instructions" [p.10]。
- 评估器：把模型输出的 [Amount] 与预期 XML 中对应标签的值逐行比较，范围是主 Form 1040 上 "the most-important lines (the lines that most impact Tax owed and balance due/refund)" [p.5]；被评估行的数量与清单原文未说明。
- 指标与基线：四项指标（strict、lenient 及各自的 by line 口径，定义见第 6 节）[p.6]。作者称 strict 是 "the only actual metric that matters in the end because the IRS expects 100%-correctly computed tax returns"，脚注 2 写 "occasionally some ambiguity with regard to rounding rules that could lead to multiple acceptable strict values" [p.6]。性能基线设为 100%，因为模型与 deterministic 'traditional code' engine 比较 [p.5]。
- thinking level 与运行次数：5 个 thinking level（定义见第 6 节），作者称目的是判断更多 thinking budget 是否对该任务有益；每个模型每档运行 4 次，用于计算 pass@k 与 pass^k [p.6]。
- 定性分析：作者对 3 个运行逐行对照官方表单（见第 4 节），选取办法原文未说明 [pp.7–9][p.14]。
- 原文未说明：完整提示词文本、temperature 等采样参数（作者在后续工作中写 "We'd like to experiment with the temperature setting of models" [p.13]）、4 次运行的汇总方式、各模型 ultrathink 档的具体 token 预算、拒答的频率 [pp.5–7]。

## 3. 数据与范围

- 规模与范围：51 个 test cases，均为 Tax Year 2024 (TY24) 的美国联邦申报表，作者称它们 "represent a modest range of personal income tax returns" [p.2]；作者举例为居住在 Florida、Texas 等无所得税州的用户 [p.4]；不含州税与地方税 [p.13]。
- 覆盖特征（作者用 'includes features like' 引出）：Filing statuses 为 Single、Married Filing Jointly、Head of Household；Income sources 为 W-2、self-employed、capital gains、interest、dividends；Credits and deductions 为 Child Tax Credit、Earned Income Tax Credit、Child and Dependent Care Expenses [p.5]。
- 来源与构造：作者所在机构为 Column Tax [p.1]；数据集取自 Column Tax 用于测试其 tax calculation engine 的测试集的一个子集，由 human experts 创建并经 deterministic tax engine 验证 [p.5]；作者称它 "crafted by hand by a team of human Tax Software Analyst experts" [p.4]。数据为合成数据，"no real taxpayer data is represented in this test data at all" [p.5]。每个测试一个目录，含 input.json 与 output.xml [p.5]；代码与数据地址见首页脚注 https://github.com/column-tax/tax-calc-bench [p.1]。
- 评测的模型：Gemini 2.5 Pro、Gemini 2.5 Flash、Claude Opus 4、Claude Sonnet 4 [p.5]，标识符为 gemini-2.5-pro-preview-05-06、gemini-2.5-flash-preview-05-20、claude-opus-4-20250514、claude-sonnet-4-20250514 [p.11]；作者写只测试知识截止日期在 2025 年的模型，理由是这些模型对 2024 税年有完整信息 [p.10]。每个模型在 5 个 thinking level 下各运行 4 次，每次覆盖 51 个 test cases [p.6][p.11]。
- 基线：作者设定为 100%，对象是 deterministic 的 'traditional code' engine [p.5]。

- 作者对 Gemini 2.5 Pro 的描述：它是 "the best-performing model on this benchmark" [p.10]，并写 "model performance does not increase for Gemini 2.5 Pro above a certain thinking budget" [p.10]。
- 作者对 Gemini 2.5 Flash 的描述：该模型 "requires at least some thinking budget to get improved performance" [p.10]；在 ultrathink 档它尝试拆出并计算 Form 8962 与 Schedule 2，较低档 "often won't do this" [p.8]。
- 作者对 Claude 的描述："Claude's Opus and Sonnet models see greatly improved performance with increased thinking budgets" [p.10]；Claude Opus 4 是第二名，但 "still lags in performance behind Gemini 2.5 Pro at lower thinking budget levels" [p.10]；Claude 的 API 默认关闭 thinking budget [p.10]。
- pass^k：作者写 "Scores reliably decrease as we increase k in the pass^k metric" [p.6]；p.12 折线图的图题为 pass^k Metric (strict) – Best Thinking Level per Model，横轴 k 取 1 至 4，四个模型各一条线，自 k=1 到 k=4 均向下，图中未印出各点数值 [p.12]。作者写 4 次运行可用于计算 pass@k 与 pass^k [p.6]，文中只给出 pass^k 的图，未给出 pass@k 的数值 [p.12]。
- 作者列出的 'some bright spots'：(a) "Models do better on the lenient metric: for many returns, the models are only a few dollars off on some lines" [p.6]；(b) "Models are better than their overall correct return results on a per line basis" [p.7]；另写 "frontier models can calculate some of the simplest returns" [p.6]；(c) 附录 A 中 Claude Sonnet 4 在 taxable social security benefits 的 Line 6b 输出 1,690，作者写 "only off by $1 from the expected amount" [p.15]。
- Child Tax Credit 与 Earned Income Tax Credit：作者写 "they reliably fail to calculate some parts of tax law, e.g. the Child Tax Credit or Earned Income Tax Credit which include complex eligibility requirements" [p.6]；原文未给出失败数量或比例。
- 失败案例一（Tax Table）：test case hoh-multiple-w2-box12-codes 中，Claude Opus 4 在 Line 15 输出应税收入 26000（`$47,900 - $21,900`），在 Line 16 输出 `Tax on $26,000 for HOH: $16,550 × 10% + $9,450 × 12%`，金额 2789；评估器记录 "Line 16: ✗incorrect, expected: 2792.0, actual: 2789.0"，作者称相差 $3，并称 IRS 期望使用 Tax Table 查找 Head of Household 在应税收入 $26,000 至 $26,050 之间的确切税额 [pp.7–8]。
- 失败案例二（Form 8962 与 Schedule 2）：作者写 "The majority of return completions attempted by the models have some sort of calculation error" [p.8]，未给出具体比例；并称 single-w2-healthcare-marketplace-1095a 一例为 "one prototypical example" [p.8]：Gemini 2.5 Flash 在 ultrathink 档错误计算 Form 8962（Premium Tax Credit），导致 Schedule 2 错误，并使申报表自 Line 17 起错误 [p.8]。作者写模型 "hallucinates incorrect line numbers on Form 8962"：Lines 1 与 2 被错误互换，Line 3 的内容也不对（模型该行输出 186.75%），并使用了错误的 Federal Poverty Level，$15,060 对 $14,580 [pp.8–9]。模型输出的 Schedule 2 为 Line 1 = 1,391、Line 2 = 158、Line 3 = 1,549、Line 21 = 0，作者称 "Which doesn't match the official Schedule 2 at all" [p.9]；p.9 图中官方 Schedule 2 的 1a、1z 与第 3 行栏内均印有 69（图中印出，正文未复述）[p.9]。
- 失败案例三（附录 A）：Claude Sonnet 4 在 single-senior-blind-over-65 的 high 档第 4 次运行，作者称该运行 "serves as a good example of models' typical performance on this task" [p.14]；作者写它正确识别了应申报的 taxable interest 金额，但又用提前支取储蓄的罚款（作者写应改在 Schedule 1 的 line 18 申报）去减 taxable interest，输出 `Line 2b: Taxable interest | Interest income less early withdrawal penalty: $1,222 - $1,212 | 10` [p.14]。

### 作者的解释或推论

- strict 与 lenient 的差距：作者认为 15-20% 的差距 "is often based on models using percentage, bracket-based calculations for Line 16 of the Form 1040 tax return"（作者称这是计算全年 total tax liability 的一行）[p.7]。作者引用 IRS 对 Form 1040 的说明：应税收入低于 $100,000 时必须使用 Tax Table，$100,000 或以上时使用 Tax Computation Worksheet，并写 "models are often ignoring this instruction by-default" [p.7]。
- 失败类型：作者写模型在两个主要方面算错，(1) 用 tax bracket percentage-based calculations 取代 lookup tables，作者标注为 "15-20% of test cases"；(2) calculation errors [p.7]。
- 逐行与整表结果的差距：作者认为 "this indicates that there are often single mistakes (discussed in more detail in the Results Analysis below) on the tax return that cascade throughout the rest of the lines, leading to incorrect returns overall" [p.7]。
- 不一致性：作者认为模型的不一致 "is not acceptable for a task which needs consistently correct results with clear auditability" [p.6]。
- thinking budget：对 Gemini 2.5 Pro 在更高档位没有提升，作者写 "This indicates that above that thinking budget, the model is not spending its thinking tokens on anything that can improve its performance" [p.10]；在 Discussion 中称这是 'initial hypothesis'："the model either confuses itself further or has no resources to spend its thinking budget on anything additionally useful" [p.13]。对 Claude，作者写默认关闭 thinking budget "significantly hampers Claude's performance on this benchmark" [p.10]。
- 对 scaffolding 的设想：作者写 "We can think of methods that could augment the models in order to force them to use the proper Tax Tables" [p.8]；结果 "point to the need for additional scaffolding, infrastructure, and augmentation that could potentially help the models compute some of the additional forms" [p.10]；并写 "getting this right will be tricky" [p.10]。
- 附录 A 中 $1 的差异：作者写 "possibly due to a rounding issue" [p.15]；并写完整计算依赖 IRS Publication 915 的 worksheet，是 "a more-involved multi-party calculation" [p.15]。

## 5. 作者陈述的局限与前提

- 任务前提：输入含计算申报表所需的全部信息，Document collection 与 Preparation 被视为已 100% 正确完成 [p.4]。
- 数据集范围：作者写 TY24 版 "is a slimmed-down version of the true complexity of the task"：仅联邦层面（作者写 '42 states + D.C. levy state/local income tax'），只涵盖相对简单的税务情形（'the vast majority of tax forms are not covered by this dataset'），不要求 MeF schema 兼容的 XML 输出 [p.13]；p.2 写这些申报表 "representing just a share of Americans' tax situations" [p.2]。
- 输出与评分：简化文本输出的理由见 p.5 脚注 1 [p.5]；strict 下舍入规则的歧义可能导致多个可接受的值 [p.6]；lenient 的 +/- $5 标准，作者写 "not allowed in tax calculation, but interesting nonetheless" [p.2]。
- 评测范围：只测试知识截止日期在 2025 年的模型 [p.10]。
- 提示词：作者称所选提示是自认为公平的一个 [p.7]，并希望再做 prompt engineering，看能否减少 tax table usage 与 calculation errors 两类失败 [p.13]。
- 尚未做的分析：作者写希望 "spend additional time analyzing why additional thinking budget does not increase performance for the top-tier Gemini 2.5 Pro model" [p.13]；temperature 设置及其对 pass^k 的影响尚未实验 [p.13]。
- 后续版本：作者预计每年发布一个版本，未来版本将加入州申报表和更复杂的情形，并改为对 proper XML 输出进行测试 [p.13]。

## 6. 关键术语

- TaxCalcBench：作者提出的基准，用于 "determining models' abilities to calculate personal income tax returns given all of the necessary information" [p.1]，由 51 对 user inputs 与预期正确输出及 testing harness 组成 [p.4]。
- tax engine：作者写 "Behind the scenes of that tax filing software is a 'tax engine' that computes the income, tax liability, credits, etc. that make up your tax return" [p.2]；传统的 tax calculation engines 是 deterministic software [p.2]；用户的全年财务数据与事实在软件中称 inputs [p.2]。
- Correct returns (strict) 与 (lenient)：strict 指每个被评估行的金额都严格一致；lenient 指每个被评估行都在预期值 +/- $5 之内。Correct (by line) 与 (by line, lenient)：分别为被评估行中与预期值一致、在预期值 +/- $5 之内的百分比 [p.6]。
- thinking levels：lobotomized 指 "either no thinking token budget or the lowest thinking budget allowed by the model"；low、medium、high 分别写作 translates to OpenAI's reasoning_effort at 1024、2048、4096 budget thinking tokens；ultrathink 指 "the highest thinking token budget allowed by the model" [p.6]。
- pass@k 与 pass^k：每个模型每档运行 4 次，据此计算 [p.6]；作者写 pass^k 是 "a metric of reliability over several runs defined by the τ-bench paper" [p.6]；pass@k 取自 HumanEval，pass^k 取自 TAU-bench [p.13]；原文未给出公式。
- MeF XML：IRS 期望的 'Modernized e-File (MeF)' XML 格式，用于预期输出 [p.4]；模型输出为简化文本格式 [p.5]。
- Tax Table：作者引用 IRS 对 Form 1040 的说明，应税收入低于 $100,000 时 "you must use the Tax Table"，$100,000 或以上时使用 Tax Computation Worksheet [p.7]。

## 7. 原文摘录

- "Our experiment shows that state-of-the-art models succeed in calculating less than a third of federal income tax returns even on this simplified sample set." [p.1]
- "Our analysis concludes that models consistently misuse tax tables, make errors in tax calculation, and incorrectly determine eligibility." [p.1]
- "Model outputted returns are considered correct if the amounts strictly match for every evaluated line. This is the only actual metric that matters in the end because the IRS expects 100%-correctly computed tax returns" [p.6]
- "Models are inconsistent in their calculations - This is not acceptable for a task which needs consistently correct results with clear auditability. Scores reliably decrease as we increase k in the pass^k metric." [p.6]
- "The 15-20% delta between strictly Correct returns and Correct returns scored on the lenient metric (+/- $5 difference allowed per-line) is often based on models using percentage, bracket-based calculations for Line 16 of the Form 1040 tax return." [p.7]
- "Interestingly, model performance does not increase for Gemini 2.5 Pro above a certain thinking budget. ... By default, Claude’s API has thinking budgets disabled, which significantly hampers Claude’s performance on this benchmark." [p.10]
- "The TY24 edition of TaxCalcBench is a slimmed-down version of the true complexity of the task: ... It covers only a relatively simple set of tax situations: the vast majority of tax forms are not covered by this dataset" [p.13]
- "Our initial hypothesis is that the model either confuses itself further or has no resources to spend its thinking budget on anything additionally useful." [p.13]
