# The British Nationality Act as a logic program

## 1. 研究问题与作者主张

- **研究问题与目标。** 作者把 British Nationality Act 1981 的大部分条文翻译成 Prolog 所基于的逻辑形式，并说明该译本可由增强的 Prolog 系统作为程序执行，从而机械地确定该法的后果 [p.1]；作者称这是 "an experiment to test the suitability of Prolog logic for expressing and applying legislation" [p.1]。作者说明没有处理更宽泛、难得多的 simulating legal reasoning 问题，只集中于把规则机械地应用于个案这一较有限的目标，并称不意在暗示其他可执行的形式化方法不能取得类似结果 [pp.2–3]。
- **关于实现的主张。** 作者写道 Prolog 逻辑 "severely restricted"，但 "sufficiently high level"，使实现 "could resemble the style and structure of the actual text of the act"；作者认为这种相似有助于提高对实现准确性的信心，并使实现在立法变化、判例补充立法时更易维护 [p.1]。作者报告，他们 "somewhat to our surprise" 发现强调与原文相似、很少强调效率的 "declarative" 表示在实践中 "actually performed acceptably well" [p.2]。
- **关于用途的主张。** 作者报告能推导 "a limited number of more general consequences of the act"，称这种能力 "potentially quite important"：可执行的、基于逻辑的规则表示除用来应用规则外，还可辅助起草和修订规则，Layman Allen 早在 1957 年就提出过这一点 [p.2]。作者认为以计算机可执行的逻辑形式表示规则的许多 potential advantages 与是否实际使用计算机无关（如识别并消除 unintended ambiguity and imprecision）；立法形式化可能对计算技术本身有贡献；管理机构和组织的规则与法律条文有 "exactly the same character"，因而提示一种构建数据处理软件的 "unconventional approach"，例如 payroll system 可以（could）直接基于 tax and sick-pay legislation [p.2]。
- **结论中的主张。** 作者称该实验 "has largely served its purpose, at least for the time being"，并称它 "demonstrated the feasibility and promise of applying Prolog logic to a potentially large class of applications dealing with the implementation of rules and regulations"；同时称该法 "proved to be a rich source of problems for the use of logic for knowledge representation"，尤其是 negation as failure 与 counterfactual conditionals [p.16]。

## 2. 方法

- **选择该法的理由（作者陈述）。** 当初有争议并引入若干新公民类别，作者希望形式化可能阐明引起争议的一些问题；该法相对自成一体，大体没有使模拟法律推理更难的复杂因素；原始实现时（summer 1983）尚不受 case law 影响 [p.1]。
- **逻辑形式。** 知识用 definite Horn clauses 表示：A if B1 and ... Bn，一个结论、零个或多个条件；事实可视为没有条件的 degenerate rules；每组 definite Horn clauses 是一个 Prolog 程序 [p.3]。作者写道 Horn clause logic 本身 "not entirely adequate for representing legislation in a natural manner"，在许多情形下需扩展为允许条件被否定，称之为 extended Horn clause logic [pp.3–4]；它不允许 disjunctive 或 negative conclusions [p.10]。
- **翻译与结构分析。** 以 Section 1.-(1) 为例，作者先给出第一近似规则（x is a British citizen if x was born in the U.K. and ... z is a parent of x and z is a British citizen on date y），其中 "after commencement" 指 after or on the date on which the act comes into force，并以中间谓词 "x has a parent who qualifies under 1.1 on date y" 合并该条 (a)(b) 两款 [p.3]。作者先尝试 structured systems analysis 的 data-flow diagrams，发现其 "too procedural for our needs"，改用 and/or graphs，作图时 "ignored many of the data parameters altogether" [p.4]。
- **逐步细化（trial and error）。** Section 1.-(3) 使作者认识到先前的形式化不完整：还需确定取得公民身份所依据的条款，并更明确地处理时间；作者把结论改为 "x acquires British citizenship on date y by sect. 1.1"（及 sect. 1.2 的对应形式），并新增规则联系持有与取得（在世、取得于 y1 日、y1 与 y 之间未 ceases to be a British citizen）[pp.5–6]。后续条款引起进一步修改：Section 48（posthumous children）通过增加规则纳入；Section 47 使作者认为该法所需的是 "x is a parent of y on date z" 而非 x is a parent of y，需修改所有含 parent 的规则 [p.6]。作者写道，对程序而言这种 trial and error 会被视为 "bad programming methodology"，但提出（"It can be argued"）立法形式化更接近 program specification 甚至 systems analysis；作者以公理系统作类比：没有同样严格的办法证明公理本身，其表述 "must inevitably be a trial-and-error process" [p.7]。
- **执行与交互。** 个案事实由 APES 交互取得：APES 是用 micro-Prolog 实现的 expert-system shell，向用户提问，答案是规则与所供信息的逻辑后果，并可解释 why 与 how [p.2][p.8]。Prolog 从结论向条件向后推理，规则 A if B and C 被解释为过程；以 depth-first search 探索，次序由规则和条件的书写顺序决定；子问题可由其他规则、数据、询问用户、计算或询问专家求解 [p.9]。作者认为向后推理鼓励 "top-down, goal-directed knowledge representation"，使高层定义可在低层概念尚未定义时检验，称这与公理系统常规的 bottom-up 做法 "a complete reversal"，并指出后者难以确定最底层 primitive concepts [pp.9–10]。
- **否定、counterfactual、酌情条款与 vagueness 的处理。** 例外条款（Section 11.-(1)、11.-(2)）用新谓词表示例外并以 not[...] 引用，按 negation as failure 解释 [p.10]。Section 1.-(2) 的双重否定：第一个 not 按 negation as failure，第二个按 ordinary classical negation，改写为等价的肯定谓词，余下的否定信息作为用户输入 [pp.11–12]。counterfactual conditionals 用附加的 alternative rules 表示；Clause 3-(4) 的酌情权用标准与酌情两条规则表示 [p.13]。vagueness（如 "being a good character"）的做法是 "assume that the vague concepts always apply" 并生成 qualified answers；作者 "deliberately avoided" 更复杂的做法 [pp.1–2]。

## 3. 数据与范围

- **文本与范围。** British Nationality Act 1981（参考文献 [20]：Her Majesty's Stationery Office，Chapter 61，London，1981）[p.17]；被翻译的是前四个 Part、Part 5 中的定义和 Parts 1–4 所需的 schedules，approximately 50 pages of the 73-page act；未翻译部分 "consist mostly of descriptions of amendments to other acts, repeals, offenses, and proceedings related to them, and decisions involving exercise of discretion by the Secretary of State" [p.14]。
- **人员、时间与平台。** July and August 1983 由 a student, Fariba Sadri 译出，"without any expert legal assistance"；系统（含 APES）用 micro-Prolog 实现；形式化完成时可用 micro-Prolog 的是 small Z-80-based microcomputers [p.14]。
- **测试与数据。** 个案事实由用户在 APES 交互中提供 [p.8]；作者说只做了 "a limited number of test applications"，主要测试各种 real and hypothetical individuals 的公民身份 [p.2]。原文未说明测试个案的数量、来源和结果汇总，也未报告基准或定量评测指标。
- **范围边界。** 涉及先前立法（如 Immigration Act 1971 下的 right of abode）的子问题需要先前立法的知识，作者称可由人类专家或该法的计算机形式化提供 [p.9]。

## 4. 结果

**实证结果（作者报告）**

- **系统规模与运行。** 形式化完成时只有该法的一小部分能与 APES 一起装入当时的 Z-80-based microcomputers，1983 年 October 和 November 的大部分工作用于克服空间限制；As of December 1983，系统在 128 kbytes 内存的微型计算机上运行该法相对自成一体的一部分，approximately 150 rules，涉及 British citizenship 的取得 [p.14]。作者估计 512 kbytes 的 micro-Prolog 系统足以运行完整的法（about 500 rules）；写作时尚未把形式化移到更大的系统 [p.14]。
- **示例对话。** Figure 4 是 APES 对话摘录：系统就 Peter 于 16 Jan 1984 是否依某条款为 British citizen 向用户提出一系列问题，最后确认 Peter 于该日依 sect. 1.1 是 British citizen，并可按 why / how 展示推导；程序预置了该法于 January 1, 1983 生效的信息 [pp.7–9]。
- **表现与 Prolog 的限制。** 作者报告 "declarative" 表示 "actually performed acceptably well in practice"；对话质量对规则与条件的书写顺序敏感，调整上只花了 "Relatively little effort" [p.2]。Prolog 因效率方面的限制 "sometimes goes into infinite loops" 并 "fails to prove theorems that are logically implied by the axioms"，但仍可证明 "a large class of theorems" [p.8]。
- **imprecision、ambiguity 与近似。** 作者称 "we found fewer such examples than we originally expected"，且在存在 imprecision 或 ambiguity 之处 "it was usually possible to identify the intended interpretation with little difficulty"；所举 imprecision 的例子是该法未提及某人实际成为 British citizen 的时间 [p.2]。形式化假定依 1.-(1) 于出生时取得、依 1.-(2) 于发现时取得，作者称后者 "only an approximation to the act"，又称其 "a reasonable approximation"，并称改为出生时取得 very easy（原文用词）[p.6]。
- **否定。** 作者不预期无法表达 disjunctive conclusions 会成为问题，称该预期得到证实；在大多数需要处理否定之处（如对例外的处理），作者称 "a straightforward interpretation of negation as failure was adequate" [p.10]。但在 Section 1.-(2)，若把第二个 not 也按 negation as failure，则对父母均未知的弃婴，系统无法得出其依 1.-(2) 取得公民身份；作者称这一结论 "exactly the opposite of what is intended by the act" [p.11]。作者称原则上可继续构造显式定义，但 "in practice, however, we could not construct such definitions"；改为把 x was not a British citizen at the time of y's birth 等否定信息作为用户输入，称这种处理 "not entirely satisfactory"，但 "a reasonable solution for most practical purposes"；采用后，父母一方或双方未知时系统立即确立弃婴的公民身份 [p.12]。
- **nonmonotonic 推理。** 作者称该法对弃婴的默认推理是 nonmonotonic：因父母未知而依 Section 1.-(2) 为 British citizen 的弃婴，若父母随后被识别且二人在其出生时既非 British citizen 也未 settled in the United Kingdom，则先前结论须撤回；作者称这种可能性 "does not seem to have been anticipated by the drafters of the act" [pp.12–13]。
- **counterfactual conditionals 与酌情条款。** 作者写道 extended Horn clause logic "is not adequate for dealing with" counterfactual conditionals，这类条件在该法中 "occur frequently"（如 Clause 14-(1)(e)）；以 alternative rules 表示其效果，作者称这 "extremely tedious"，需要彻底分析该法条文并 "substantially" 增加规则数量，且 "not entirely satisfactory"；作者称主要困难在于发现起草者的意图 [p.13]。作者提到两类实现技术：用 Prolog 的 extralogical primitives 临时修改数据库（"sacrificing the logic of the knowledge representation"），或用 object language 与 metalanguage 的 amalgamation（"logically sound"）[p.13]。该法中另有若干短语 "seemed problematic upon first reading, but were not so in practice"，例如授予 Secretary of State 酌情权的 Clause 3-(4)，其处理的代价是规则数量增加 [p.13]。
- **规模、维护与后续。** 规则增至 "hundreds of rules and many tens of predicates" 后，纳入微小修改也并不总是容易；一项修改易于纳入还是需大幅重构 "largely a matter of luck" [p.13]；作者 "see no alternative to trial-and-error formalization"（至少在处理已存在的立法时）[pp.13–14]。作者列出 Imperial College 的 Logic Programming Group 随后完成的较小项目（如 Immigration Act 1971 的一个子集、a large company's pension regulations 及相关 tax legislation）；文中未给这些项目的结果 [p.16]。

**作者的解释或推论**

- **Prolog 与定理证明。** 作者写道，该法的形式化是 axiomatic theory，其逻辑后果原则上可由 complete mechanical theorem prover 推出；Prolog 不能处理任意逻辑句子，但 "very efficient for proving certain kinds of simple theorems"（公理以 extended Horn clauses 表示）[pp.7–8]。作者称 Prolog 适合实现立法，因为它是 nondeterministic，且 "main strength" 在于建立在逻辑之上 [p.9]。
- **Closed World Assumption。** 作者称，可作 Closed World Assumption 时 negation as failure 与 classical negation 一致，常适用于例外，并 "can be implemented very easily and efficiently"；若有理由相信存在该法未涵盖的其他取得公民身份的方式，就不能作 all-embracing Closed World Assumption，须改用能处理 ordinary negation 的证明器（可能需要全部一阶逻辑推理，"substantially less efficient"）[p.10]；作者 "would nevertheless suggest" 许多情形下立法明确规定了谓词适用的全部情形，此时可安全使用 negation as failure [pp.10–11]。
- **与 expert systems 和 conventional programming 的比较。** 作者认为立法形式化 "has almost all the characteristics of an expert system"，差别在于 knowledge elicitation problem 在此 "almost entirely absent"（法律成文，不成文处有先前判例记录），其 knowledge representation problems "much less severe than the knowledge elicitation problem in general"；作者称知识以逻辑形式表示时易于理解、易于修改，并 "allows the inference procedure to interact naturally with the human user and to explain its conclusions" [p.14]。作者说 decision trees（Figure 5）可用 Cobol 或 Basic 直接实现，但它们针对预先确定的一类问题优化，"do not aim to support the derivation of arbitrary logical consequences of the rules" [p.15]。
- **与相关工作的比较。** 作者把本工作归入基于规则推理的一类，与其他把 Prolog 用于法律的项目相似，但强调两点：集中于已写成的立法的形式表示（因而可实现 "a more substantial application" 而不必处理 knowledge elicitation problem），以及尽量区分用逻辑表示立法与用 Prolog 实现，"primary commitment" 在逻辑 [pp.15–16]。与 Stamper 的 LEGOL 相比，作者称把 LEGOL 规则按 logic programming 解释可直接表达 recursion，并称本文采用 top-down、goal-directed 开发 "for purely practical reasons" [p.16]。

## 5. 作者陈述的局限与前提

- **范围。** 作者没有处理 simulating legal reasoning 的更宽泛问题 [pp.2–3]；作者称从未打算（"It was never our intention"）把该实现发展为 fully functional system，只做了有限的测试应用 [p.2]；原始实现时该法尚不受 case law 影响，vagueness 与 case law 的处理是小组 "current investigation" 的主题，更复杂的 vagueness 处理被有意避开 [pp.1–2]。
- **近似与假设。** (a) 依 1.-(1) 于出生时取得、依 1.-(2) 于发现时取得，该假定 "not explicitly stated in the act" [p.6]；(b) 假定个体有两个亲代：唯一的父亲和唯一的母亲 [p.12]；(c) not born in the U.K. 等价于 born outside the U.K.，not born after or on commencement 等价于 born before commencement [p.12]；(d) 否定信息作为用户输入的处理 "not entirely satisfactory" [p.12]；(e) Figure 4 的对话省略了 posthumous 与 illegitimate children [p.8]。
- **推理机制的限制。** negation as failure 以 Closed World Assumption 为前提，在不宜作此假定的情形下不适用 [p.10]；Prolog 有时进入无限循环 [p.8]；足够高效的 theorem provers 目前只存在于受限形式的逻辑，且在限于求出该法对个案的后果时 "behave most effectively" [p.2]；counterfactual conditionals 的处理 "not entirely satisfactory" [p.13]。
- **规模与适用条件。** 只有一小部分法条能装入当时的微型计算机，完整的法估计含 about 500 rules，尚未移到更大系统 [p.14]；作者写道没有 "correspondingly rigorous way to justify the axioms themselves" [p.7]；以逻辑表示知识的 advantages 同样适用于其他应用，"provided they are structured according to logic programming principles" [p.14]。

## 6. 关键术语

- **definite Horn clause。** A if B1 and ... Bn：一个结论 A、零个或多个条件 Bi，每个条件是 atomic relationship；每组 definite Horn clauses 是一个 Prolog 程序 [p.3]。
- **extended Horn clause logic。** 作者对允许规则条件被否定的 Horn clause logic 的称呼 [pp.3–4]；不允许 disjunctive 或 negative conclusions [p.10]。
- **negation as failure / Closed World Assumption。** "not[Q] holds when all ways of showing Q fail"；Closed World Assumption：Anything which is not known is assumed to be false [p.10]。
- **default reasoning / nonmonotonic。** 作者称 Section 1.-(2) 的最后两个条件 "seem to involve default reasoning"，形式为 infer P if fail to show "not P" [p.11]；nonmonotonic：Conclusions made by default in the absence of information to the contrary may have to be withdrawn if new information is made available later [p.12]。
- **counterfactual conditionals。** 原文未另给定义；作者以 Clause 14-(1)(e) 中 "would have done so but for his having died or ceased to be a citizen" 的措辞为例 [p.13]。
- **APES。** 用 micro-Prolog 实现的 expert-system shell，向用户生成查询，并可解释 why 与 how [p.2][p.8]。

## 7. 原文摘录

- "Our implementation of the British Nationality Act 1981 was undertaken as an experiment to test the suitability of Prolog logic for expressing and applying legislation." [p.1]
- "It was never our intention to develop the implementation of the act into a fully functional system." [p.2]
- "We discovered, somewhat to our surprise, that our 'declarative' representation of the act, which places primary emphasis on resemblance to the original form of the legislation and very little emphasis on efficiency, actually performed acceptably well in practice." [p.2]
- "The last two conditions of this rule contain double negations, which would cancel each other out if both were interpreted classically: not[not P] is equivalent to P. Obviously, this is not what is intended by the act." [p.11]
- "In practice, however, we could not construct such definitions; the act is too large, and there are too many separate possibilities to consider for this to be a practical solution." [p.12]
- "So, although we managed to represent the effect of counterfactual conditions within extended Horn clause logic, this treatment is not entirely satisfactory." [p.13]
- "The British Nationality Act experiment has largely served its purpose, at least for the time being. It has demonstrated the feasibility and promise of applying Prolog logic to a potentially large class of applications dealing with the implementation of rules and regulations." [p.16]
