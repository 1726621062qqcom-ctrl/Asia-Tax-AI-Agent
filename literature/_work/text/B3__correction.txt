CORRECTION
Correction: Navigating optimal treaty-
shopping routes using a multiplex network
model
Sung Jae Park, Kyu-Min Lee, Jae-Suk Yang
There is an error in point 3 of the numbered list in the Previous literature on tax avoidance
subsection of the Introduction. The correct statement is: The authors assumed that the inter-
mediate station in a given network route includes only one or two countries [17, 19]. One
recent study [20] expanded the scope of its analysis of investment (ownership) structures to
three countries and van’t Riet and Lejour [18] expanded it to four intermediate countries.
However, in reality, an intermediate station in a tax treaty network can include any number of
countries.
